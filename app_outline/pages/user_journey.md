# User Journey: Netflix clone

**What it is:** A personalized Netflix clone centered around my movies and tv shows. Users login, search for movies or tv shows/series by title or key word. Or, users can filter by clicking on categories, movies or tv shows.

**Problem:** Display and organize my tv shows and movies and a short description of the plot.

**Who will use this application?** One type: a person wanting to watch tv shows or movies. There are no accounts yet, so everyone who opens the app sees the same lists. Just me. This only needs one user type. Nothing too complex.

## The journey

1. **Opens the webpage** → lands on **Home Page**. Once logged in, the home page appears, and at the top of the page and centered, from left to right appear the words Home, Movies, TV Shows, Categories with a drop-down list of the categories, and finally a search bar. Right under this top bar, appears 
"              Welcome to FleurDeLysFlix 
     Unlimited Movies & TV Shows.  Anytime. Anywhere. "  The word FleurDeLysFlix is scarlet red color.
2. **Clicks on "Movies"** at the top of the page → goes to **Movies**. The results of Movies search appear with 20 modal images per page appear in rows of 5 by columns of 4 with the number of pages of results listed at the center of the bottom of the page.
3. **Clicks on "TV Shows"** at the top of the page → goes to **TV Shows**. The results of TV Shows search appear with 20 modal images per page appear in rows of 5 by columns of 4 with the number of pages of results listed at the center of the bottom of the page.
4. **Clicks on "Categories"** at the top of the page → opens a drop list under **Categories**. Once a listed category is clicked on, the results of the category search appear with 20 modal images per page appear in rows of 5 by columns of 4 with the number of pages of results listed at the center of the bottom of the page.
4. **Entering movie/tv show title and clicks on "search bar icon"** at the top of the page → goes to the search results for the entered title, which appear appear with 20 modal images per page appear in rows of 5 by columns of 4 with the number of pages of results listed at the center of the bottom of the page.
5. **Clicks on "modal movie image"** go to chosen movie.  Chosen movie title appears with arrow play button with viewing screen in background.

## Features
- Choice of categories/genres, user is able to pick from a list, the selection will filter the movies and shows by that category/genre.
- For each show or movie, it should have an image depicting it, and a short description within a card.
- Hovering over the card highlights it, clicking on the card opens it in a larger modal, the full description is displayed here.
