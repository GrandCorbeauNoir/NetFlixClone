# Movie/tv show viewing: 



## On this page

- **FleurDeLysFlix** logo should appear in scarlet red at the top, left side of page.
- **Movies or TV Shows** The results of a Movies or TV Shows search appear with 20 modal images per page appear in rows of 5 by columns of 4 with the number of pages of results listed at the center of the bottom of the page with the number of pages of results listed at the center of the bottom of the page.
- **Movie viewing screen** Chosen movie title appears with arrow play button with viewing screen in background below the title.
**Navigation Bar** At the top of the page and centered, from left to right appear the clickable words Home, Movies, TV Shows, Categories with a drop-down list of the categories, and finally a search bar. Clicking on any of the words or the search bar icon performs a search and the results of the search will appear with 20 modal images per page appear in rows of 5 by columns of 4 with the number of pages of results listed at the center of the bottom of the page.
